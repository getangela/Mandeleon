User accounts & personal boards

  • ✓
    Visitor accounts (sign up / log in) Done Live and deployed: a Postgres schema (Netlify Database/Neon) with users, avatars, and saved_tiles tables; five Netlify Functions (avatar-get, avatar-save, tiles-sync, claim-avatar, avatar-name-check) enforcing globally-unique avatar names; and Netlify Identity wired into the avatar panel (log in/sign up/log out row, plus an optional "bring your existing avatar into this account?" prompt for anyone who already had one on their device). Real signups, logins, and avatar claims have been tested live on mandeleon.com. Personal boards below build directly on this. The one open issue is tracked separately right below — outbound confirmation emails aren't currently arriving.
  • Fix outbound confirmation emails (Resend SMTP) Waiting on Netlify support New signups create the account correctly, but the confirmation email itself isn't arriving. Root-caused to the outbound SMTP connection: Resend's domain is fully verified (DKIM/SPF/DMARC all green) and the SMTP host/port/username/password are all confirmed correctly saved in Netlify Identity's settings, tried on both port 465 and 587 — but Resend's own delivery logs show zero incoming connection attempts on any test, which points at something on Netlify's sending side rather than the Resend config. A support ticket is open with Netlify; this needs their response before there's anything more to try from our end.
  • Custom-built login/signup form (replace Netlify's popup) Requested: a "show password" eye icon on the sign-up/login password field. Not possible with the current setup — that field lives inside Netlify Identity's own hosted popup (technically a separate embedded mini-site), and browsers block a site from reaching into and modifying another company's embedded content like that, so no CSS/JS trick from our side can add a button to it. The real fix is building a fully custom sign-up/login form (styled to match the site, with its own password-reveal toggle and any other niceties) that talks to the same Netlify Identity backend directly, instead of relying on Netlify's built-in popup UI. This is a real project — designing and building the form, then wiring it to the existing auth endpoints — not a quick fix, so it's tracked here rather than attempted inline.

Gamification / Engagement Optional, needs accounts

  • Virtual "Mandeleon" avatar system A Tamagotchi-style personal avatar visitors name and keep healthy by "feeding" it real product, video, and article tiles from the site. Health meters map to your actual categories (Brain, Sleep & Stress, Energy & Cellular Repair, Heart, Gut & Liver, Immune & Defense, Muscle & Hormones, Skin), decay daily, and are restored by browsing and feeding. Core mechanics (decay rates, feed values, daily caps, discovery and affiliate bonuses) are built and live, along with creation, leveling, vitality tiers, Health/Pool status, biological age, and the adjustable photo crop. Both polish details originally flagged for later are also now done: (1) a subtle green glow around the avatar when most (not all) meters are green — a milder everyday version of the golden Peak glow, which still only fires when every meter is maxed — and (2) a confetti burst alongside the existing "✨ Perfect Feed!" toast for the random bonus-feed moments. The one piece still genuinely outstanding is the global leaderboard comparing everyone's avatar (Cohort Pools, Section 8 of the gamification spec) — that needs the visitor accounts/database work above, since it requires seeing other people's avatars.
  • ✓
    Create and provide a full ladder of avatar poses Done Live: 6 distinct hand-drawn poses, one per Health-status tier — Peak (victory pose, every meter at 100), Robust (biceps-flex, every meter 70+), Normal (hands-on-hips, average 50+ — also the starting pose for a freshly created avatar), Weak (arms-at-sides, average 25–49), Fading (slumped/head-down, average below 25), and Coma (kneeling/collapsed — see the Coma trigger & Revival item below, also now done). The uniform itself also still visibly degrades tier over tier (progressive desaturation) on top of each pose's own baked-in wear — the grime-texture overlay originally used for this was replaced with the Vitality Halo below. Cohort Pool was renamed to "Class" at the same time (Elite / Optima / Basic / Grunt) to avoid clashing with Health status's own new "Normal" tier.
  • ✓
    Vitality halo (replaces the grime-texture overlay) Done The uniform's grime/dirt texture from neglect didn't read well, so it's been replaced with a glow that traces the avatar's actual silhouette instead of sitting on the fabric as dirt. Same idea across all 6 Health tiers — it shrinks, cools in color, and slows its pulse as health drops: biggest and brightest gold at Peak, a large green aura at Robust, a medium pale blue-white aura at Normal, a faded burnt-orange outline at Weak (thicker than Fading's, but very see-through, so it doesn't compete with Peak's gold), a thin, soft red glow at Fading, and no halo at all in Coma. Built with layered SVG filters so the glow genuinely hugs whichever pose is showing rather than floating behind it as a plain circle.
  • ✓
    Coma trigger & Revival flow Done Live: per gamification-spec.md Section 4, a 72-real-hour countdown starts the moment the meter average first drops below 25 (Fading) — feeding back above 25 before the countdown finishes cancels it with no penalty, otherwise the avatar enters Coma. Meters stop decaying further once there (no piling-on penalty), and normal feeding is disabled — feeding instead counts toward Revival, which triggers automatically once 4 different meter categories have been fed, waking the avatar at a low ~20-25 baseline per meter (not full health — real effort is still needed afterward, but per the spec nothing is ever lost permanently). The avatar panel shows a dedicated banner with live Revival progress while in Coma, plus a one-time toast the moment the transition is first discovered (whether that's opening the avatar panel or just feeding a tile from the grid). Note: this checks are lazy/client-side, same as the existing daily decay — there's no server-side timer, so the transition is caught the next time the app happens to be open, exactly like decay already works.
  • Avatar photo moderation/approval structure Today, a visitor's uploaded avatar face photo lives only in their own browser (localStorage) and is never seen by anyone else, so there's nothing to moderate yet. That changes once avatars move to a shared backend for cross-device sync and the Cohort Pools leaderboard — the point where other visitors can actually see each other's avatars. At that point, uploaded photos need a real review structure before (or immediately after) they're visible to others: e.g. a "Pending" state on any new/changed photo until approved, reusing the same Pending Submissions admin queue and Approve/Reject pattern already planned for product/content submissions, plus a post-publish "flag this photo" safety net for the community to surface anything missed. Tied to the Foundation-phase Track A milestone (avatar state + face images moving to backend storage) in the gamification spec — not a Phase 1 concern since no one else can see these photos yet.
