⬇ Publish changes

Tiles you add/edit/delete here save immediately to the database, but the live site doesn't change until you export a fresh index.html and upload it — same delete-and-drag-and-drop method you already use. This keeps mandeleon.com exactly as fast and static as it's always been.

⚠️ The download will be named mandeleon-export.html, not index.html — on purpose, so it can never silently collide with an old download and get auto-renamed by your browser (that's exactly what caused a real outage once before). Rename it to index.html yourself before uploading it.
